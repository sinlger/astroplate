## Complete Instagram Image Sizes for 2026

### Profile Picture
- **Recommended Size**: 320 × 320 pixels
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1 (square)
- **Notes**: Instagram always crops profile pictures to a circle, so keep your face/logo centered.

### Square Feed Post
- **Recommended Size**: 1080 × 1080 pixels
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1
- **Best For**: Product photos, graphic quotes, grids

### Portrait Feed Post
- **Recommended Size**: 1080 × 1350 pixels
- **Aspect Ratio**: 4:5
- **Best For**: Most feed posts, vertical photos, portraits
- **Note**: This is currently the most popular format because it takes up more space in the feed.

### Landscape Feed Post
- **Recommended Size**: 1080 × 608 pixels
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9
- **Best For**: Landscapes, wide group photos, horizontal video thumbnails

### Instagram Stories
- **Recommended Size**: 1080 × 1920 pixels
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16
- **Best For**: Stories, Reels covers

### Reels Thumbnail
- **Recommended Size**: 1080 × 1920 pixels
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16

### Instagram Carousel
- **Recommended Size**: Same as single posts above
- **Aspect Ratio**: All slides should have the same aspect ratio for best results

### IGTV Cover
- **Recommended Size**: 420 × 654 pixels
- **Aspect Ratio**: 1:1.556

Instagram recommends that all photos be at least 1080 pixels wide. If you upload a photo wider than 1080 pixels, Instagram will resize it down to 1080 pixels. If you upload smaller than 320 pixels wide, it will be enlarged, which can cause blurriness.

### Does higher resolution mean better quality on Instagram?
