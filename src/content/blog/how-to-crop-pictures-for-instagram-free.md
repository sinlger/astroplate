## What Aspect Ratios Does Instagram Allow?

Instagram supports three main aspect ratios for feed posts:

| Aspect Ratio | Best For | Output Size |
|--------------|----------|-------------|
| **1:1 Square** | Product shots, single subject photos | 1080 × 1080 px |
| **4:5 Portrait** | Most popular for feed posts, vertical photos | 1080 × 1350 px |
| **16:9 Landscape** | Wide landscapes, group photos | 1080 × 608 px |
| **9:16 Stories / Reels** | Stories, Reels covers | 1080 × 1920 px |

## Why Crop Before Uploading to Instagram?

If you don't crop your photo to the correct aspect ratio before uploading:
- Instagram will automatically crop it for you, often cutting off important parts of your image
- You have less control over the final composition
- Automatic cropping can lead to lower quality if Instagram has to resize your image unnecessarily

By cropping yourself before uploading, you get exactly the composition you want.

## Tips for Better Instagram Cropping

1. **Leave breathing room around your subject**: Don't crop important elements too tight to the edge
2. **Use the rule of thirds**: Position your main subject at one of the intersecting grid lines
3. **Check the preview**: Always see how your cropped image will look before downloading
4. **Export at full resolution**: We export at 1080px width which is Instagram's recommended resolution
