## Pro Tips for All Social Media Images

1. **Always use the recommended resolution**: Higher resolution images look sharper on high-DPI (Retina) displays
2. **Mind the file size**: Most platforms compress images, but excessively large files slow down loading
3. **Check mobile visibility**: Important content should stay within the safe zone to avoid cropping on mobile
4. **Test before posting**: Resize your image to the correct dimensions *before* uploading — don't let the platform do it for you
